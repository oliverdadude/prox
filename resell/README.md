# 📦 Resell Profit Checker

Work out whether something from Sainsbury's, Tesco, Asda, Boots and other shops will make a profit on Amazon UK before you buy it.

- Enter the shop price, the Amazon price, the category and the size. You get profit per item, ROI, margin, break-even price and the most you can pay to hit your target ROI.
- Allows for Amazon referral fees, FBA fees, storage, postage, prep and VAT (registered or not).
- One-click links to search Amazon UK, Keepa (sales and price history) and the shop.
- Save products to a list, track them from Idea → Bought → Listed → Sold, and export or import CSV.
- Runs entirely in the browser. Your list is saved on your device only.

Fee figures are estimates. Check them against Amazon's Revenue Calculator before buying in bulk, and use "Override Amazon fees" to enter the exact numbers.

## Run it

Open `index.html`, or host the folder anywhere static (GitHub Pages, Vercel).
