Before you buy: checklist

  1. Can you sell in that category? On Amazon UK, Grocery & Gourmet and many big brands are gated (they need approval). Amazon usually wants wholesale invoices for approval, and supermarket receipts are normally rejected. In Seller Central, search the product and check it says you can list it.
  2. Does it actually sell? Open the Keepa link. Look at sales rank (lower is better) and how many sell each month. A big profit on something that sells once a month ties up your money.
  3. Is Amazon itself the seller? If Amazon sells it, you'll rarely win the Buy Box. Count the other FBA sellers too.
  4. Use a price that holds. Use the usual Buy Box price, not a one-day spike. Keepa's 90-day average is a safer number.
  5. Check the dates. FBA needs dated items to have plenty of shelf life left when they arrive (usually at least 90 days). Clearance stock near its date won't be accepted.
  6. Match the listing exactly. Same size, same pack count, same variant. Multipacks and "bundles" have strict rules.
  7. Keep every receipt for your accounts and in case a brand complains.

Fees here are estimates for Amazon UK. Amazon changes them, so before you buy in bulk, confirm with Amazon's own Revenue Calculator and paste its figures into "Override Amazon fees".
