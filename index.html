🎙️ My Spotify broadcast

Play anything on Spotify and the radio plays it too. Your PC streams Spotify's sound to your own station, and you paste one link into the radio. Set it up once with the checklist below.

Setup checklist 0/9

Heads-up: Spotify only allows personal listening, so rebroadcasting it to a server breaks their terms of use. That's a risk to your Spotify account.

No sound? Check that the BUTT level meter moves while Spotify plays. If it doesn't, Spotify isn't set to CABLE Input. Echo or game sounds in the stream? BUTT is recording your whole PC, so set its device to CABLE Output. Radio rejects the link? Try it with http:// instead of https://. Expect a 5–30 second delay, and keep BUTT and Spotify running.

📻 Non-stop radio stations

The car radio only plays direct audio streams (.mp3 / .pls / .m3u), not YouTube or Spotify links. Pick a station, hit Copy link, paste it into the radio and press Enter. It keeps playing, no more links needed.
